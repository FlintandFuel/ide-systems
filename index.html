Paxiss Feature Catalogue

Everything a CFI needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Paxiss covers the full operational surface of a regulated cooperative financial institution, built so your team can run confidently from day one.

Client Profile Management

Capture and maintain comprehensive client profile information, including personal details, contact information, identification records, account relationships, and regulatory data.

Account Management

Open, maintain, and manage accounts for all product types offered by the institution. Account functionality is driven by product definitions and client eligibility criteria.

Transaction Processing Engine

A comprehensive engine supporting deposits, withdrawals, transfers, payments, and fund transfers to external institutions. Also supports sundry transactions between general ledger accounts, as well as suspended, deferred, and automated transactions.

Standing Instructions

Automate recurring client transactions based on predefined schedules. Instructions can continue indefinitely or terminate automatically when specified end criteria are met.

Cash Management

Manage and control cash holdings issued to officials from the treasury. Tracks cash balances by denomination, records all movements, and supports end-of-day balancing and reconciliation. Multiple teller positions with full support for cash transfers between tellers and the treasury.

Product Definition Engine

A flexible, user-definable product configuration facility that enables the institution to create and maintain unique financial products across various product classes, including savings, loans, investments, and transactional accounts.

Interest Management

Interest structures are dynamically configurable on a per-product basis. Define and maintain interest calculation methods, rates, and related parameters for each product, allowing the system to automatically calculate and apply interest according to product-specific rules.

Fee Management

Fees are fully user-definable, allowing authorised officials to configure fee structures and the rules governing their application. The system automatically calculates and applies the appropriate fees to transactions.

General Ledger Management

A fully integrated general ledger facility automatically records all accounting entries arising from transactions, ensuring complete financial integrity and real-time financial reporting.

Sundry Transactions

Authorised and mandated officials can perform controlled financial transactions directly between general ledger accounts, supporting operational and administrative financial activities.

Shareholding Management

Manage member shareholdings, including the issuance, maintenance, transfer, and redemption of shares. The system also supports the printing and management of share certificates.

Relationship Management

Capture and maintain relationships between clients, accounts, and related parties, such as representatives, signatories, trustees, beneficiaries, and guardians. All compliance requirements applicable to clients are automatically enforced for related parties.

Pledge Management

Manage and track pledged (ceded) accounts, including the registration, maintenance, and monitoring of third-party cessions and associated account restrictions.

FICA Management

Comprehensive FICA management for all profiles, including clients and related parties. FICA-related documentation is stored electronically within the system for compliance, verification, and audit purposes.

CODI Compliance

Supports all data capture, validation, reporting, and submission requirements necessary to comply with the South African Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI) regulations.

Regulatory Data Management

Ensures the capture and maintenance of all information required to meet regulatory, governance, and compliance obligations.

Document Management

Integrated document management for storing and managing client-related documents, including contracts, wills, trust deeds, identification documents, share certificates, and other supporting records directly within the system.

Electronic Record Retention

Secure storage and retrieval of supporting documentation and historical records, providing a complete audit trail for regulatory and operational purposes.

Security and Access Control

Role-based security enables administrators to control access to functions, data, and transactions based on user roles and responsibilities.

Mandate Management

Allows authorised officials to temporarily delegate specific rights and responsibilities to other officials under controlled and auditable conditions.

Audit and Forensic Tracking

Comprehensive audit and forensic tracking records all user activities, transactions, approvals, amendments, and system access. The system maintains a complete audit trail showing who performed each action, when it was performed, and from which workstation or facility.

Operational Monitoring

Provides visibility into user activity, system usage, transaction history, and operational events to support governance, oversight, and risk management.

Dynamically switch the system between English and Afrikaans, with all screens, menus, messages, and reports displayed in the selected language.
