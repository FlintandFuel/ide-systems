PAXISS Feature Catalogue

Comprehensive functionality. Purpose-built for co-operative banking.

PAXISS brings together the core banking functionality required by regulated co-operative financial institutions and banks in a single integrated platform, enabling your team to manage day-to-day banking operations efficiently, securely, and with confidence.

Client Profile Management

Capture and maintain comprehensive client profiles, including personal and contact details, identification records, relationships with related parties, and all relevant regulatory and compliance information.

Account Management

Open, maintain, and manage accounts for all product types offered by the institution. Account functionality is driven by product definitions and client eligibility criteria.

Transaction Processing Engine

A comprehensive transaction management system supporting deposits, withdrawals, transfers, payments, and fund transfers to external financial institutions. It also supports transactions between general ledger accounts, as well as suspended ('pending'), deferred, scheduled, and automated transactions.

Standing Instructions

Automated recurring client transactions executed according to predefined schedules. Standing instructions may continue indefinitely or terminate automatically once specified end conditions are met.

Cash & Teller Management

Manage and control cash holdings issued from the treasury to tellers and other authorised officials. Track cash balances by denomination, record all cash movements, and support end-of-day balancing and reconciliation. Multiple teller positions are supported, including full control and tracking of cash transfers between tellers and the treasury.

Product Configuration

A flexible, user-definable product configuration facility that enables the institution to create, configure, and maintain financial products tailored to its specific requirements across multiple product classes, including savings, loans, investments, and transactional accounts.

Interest Management

Interest structures are dynamically configurable for each product. Define and maintain interest calculation methods, rates, structures, and related parameters, enabling the system to automatically calculate and apply interest in accordance with product-specific rules.

Fee Management

Fees are fully user-definable, enabling authorised officials to configure fee structures, rates, and the rules governing their application. The system automatically calculates and applies the appropriate fees in accordance with the defined rules.

General Ledger

A fully integrated general ledger automatically records all accounting entries arising from transactions, ensuring financial integrity, accurate account balances, and up-to-date financial reporting.

Sundry General Ledger Transactions

Authorised and duly mandated officials can perform controlled financial transactions directly between general ledger accounts, with appropriate controls and audit trails to support operational and administrative financial activities.

Shareholding Management

Manage member shareholdings throughout their lifecycle, including the issuance, maintenance, transfer, and redemption of shares. The system also supports the generation, printing, and management of share certificates.

Related Party Management

Capture and maintain relationships between clients, accounts, and related parties, including representatives, signatories, trustees, beneficiaries, and guardians. The system automatically applies and enforces all relevant compliance and regulatory requirements applicable to each related party.

Surety & Collateral Management

Capture and manage third-party sureties and collateral pledged as security for loans, including the registration and maintenance of ceded accounts, monitoring of pledged values, and enforcement of associated account restrictions.

FICA Compliance Management

Comprehensive FICA compliance management across all profiles, including clients and related parties. FICA-related information and supporting documentation are securely maintained electronically within the system to support compliance, verification, ongoing monitoring, and audit requirements.

CODI Regulatory Compliance

Supports the data capture, validation, reporting, and submission processes required to comply with the regulatory requirements of the South African Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI).

Regulatory & Compliance Management

Ensures the comprehensive capture and maintenance of all information required to meet applicable regulatory, governance, and compliance obligations.

Document Management

Integrated document management for the secure storage and management of client-, account-, and transaction-related documents, including contracts, wills, trust deeds, identification documents, share certificates, surety and security documentation, and other supporting records directly within the system.

Security and Access Control

Role-based security enables administrators to define and control user access to system functions, data, and transactions according to assigned roles, responsibilities, and levels of authority.

Delegation of Authority

Enables authorised officials to temporarily delegate specified access rights, authorities, and responsibilities to other officials for defined periods, subject to appropriate controls and a complete audit trail.

Audit and Forensic Tracking

Comprehensive audit and forensic tracking records all user activities, transactions, approvals, amendments, and system access. A complete and tamper-resistant audit trail identifies who performed each action, when it occurred, and the workstation or facility from which it was performed.

System Activity Monitoring

Provides comprehensive visibility into user activity, system usage, transaction history, and operational events, supporting effective governance, management oversight, compliance, and risk management.

Seamlessly switch the system between English and Afrikaans, with all screens, menus, messages, and reports automatically displayed in the selected language. The system architecture also allows for additional languages to be supported where required.

Deployment Options

Your infrastructure. Your choice.

PAXISS provides the same core banking functionality whether deployed in a managed cloud environment or on your institution's own infrastructure. Choose the deployment model that best suits your operational, security, regulatory, and IT requirements.

Managed Cloud Hosting

PAXISS can be hosted in a managed cloud environment, with infrastructure, system updates, backups, security maintenance, and availability monitoring managed on your behalf.

  • + Minimal local infrastructure requirements
  • + Managed updates and security maintenance
  • + Managed backup and recovery processes
  • + Predictable hosting and support costs
  • + Well suited to institutions with limited internal IT capacity

On-Premises Installation

PAXISS can be deployed on your institution's own servers or private infrastructure, providing greater control over the data environment, system access, infrastructure, and integration architecture.

  • + Full control over your data environment and infrastructure
  • + Greater flexibility for integration with internal systems
  • + Suitable for institutions with established IT infrastructure
  • + Supports institution-specific security and infrastructure policies

Full implementation and ongoing support

IDE Systems provides implementation, configuration, migration, training, and ongoing technical support for both deployment models.
